Shift-Click Issue Filer

Hold Shift and click any element on a page to file an issue into your workspace.

Where your issues live — not the site you're inspecting. Leave it on https://amplifier.app unless you run your own workspace, in which case use its base URL (no trailing path).
Use the token from Settings → MCP Connection in your workspace (the same one MCP uses). The token is user-level — it reaches every account you belong to; pick the account right in the composer when filing.
Connect below to load your teams. The picker follows the team you last filed into, and you can change it per-issue.
